David Byrne

Sculptor — Yukuhashi, Japan

David Byrne

I'm an Irish sculptor based in rural Japan, making objects that don't yet physically exist but can be realised at any time. I design artworks that hover between intention and object — fully realised in 3D software, waiting for the foundry, the router, the printer. The shadow, it turns out, arrives before the thing that casts it.
